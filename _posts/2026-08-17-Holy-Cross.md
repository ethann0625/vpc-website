## Performance

On August 17 2026, we came back to the Holy Cross Manor care facility for our last performance of the summer! Since we were there just a month ago, many people recognized us and greeted us. We were joined by one of our long-time volunteers, Levi, to play piano and played:

1. Fly me to the moon
1. Autumn Leaves
1. Misty
1. La Vie en Rose
1. I fall in love too easily
1. Falling behind
1. My way
1. Just the two of us
